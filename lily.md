Key DimensionsPlant Height: 30–70 cm (\(1 \text{ to } 2'6"\)).Flower Cluster (Umbel) Width: 15–25 cm.Individual Flower Length: 5–7.5 cm (2–3 inches).Stamen Length: 7.5–10 cm (3–4 inches) projecting outward.Bulb Diameter: 3–8 cm (1–3 inches).Anatomical Structure & DescriptionUnderground Bulb: Nearly spherical and covered in purplish-brown membranous scales. It remains dormant during the summer, producing dark green, strap-like leaves after the blooming cycle.Scape (Stem): A solid, leafless green stalk that emerges directly from the bulb.Umbel (Flower Head): A circular cluster of 4 to 6 individual flowers arranged at the apex of the scape.Tepals (Petals): Each flower has 6 narrow, wavy tepals that curl intensely backward, resembling the curved legs of a spider.Stamens & Pistil: The most defining feature of the plant. Six exceptionally long, elegant stamens and a single long pistil extend well beyond the curled petals, creating a radiating "spoke" effect. These are typically crimson, occasionally fading to pink or white at the tips.
